What makes it different

Built on three promises

Most tools either flatter every fund or drown you in numbers. Truthfolio does neither.

🫡

Full honesty

If a fund is weak, we say so — even if it made you money. A fund that's up can still be a bad place for your money when better options exist.

🧾

Evidence-based

Every claim traces back to a real, computed number. Expand any verdict to see its sources and exactly how much each one is trusted.

🧒

Explained simply

Every metric comes with an everyday-analogy explanation. “Was the bumpy ride worth it?” instead of “risk-adjusted return.”

What you can do

Review one fund, or your whole portfolio

🔍

Review a single fund

Search any real, registered scheme and get the full picture:

  • A clear Continue / Stop / Shift verdict, health score and confidence
  • Returns, risk and consistency — each in plain English
  • How it ranks against similar funds
  • An evidence ledger you can open to see the proof
📊

Review your portfolio

Add your holdings with units and average buy price, and see:

  • Your real gain/loss and return, per fund and overall
  • A portfolio health score and verdict
  • Where your money sits, and if you're over-concentrated
  • Your best and worst performers, and what to reconsider
