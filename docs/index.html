The Truthfolio standard

Three things we won't compromise.

Money decisions are personal. The information behind them should be clear, honest, and easy to challenge.

Honest, even when awkward.

A fund can have made money and still have weak spots. The review weighs both instead of telling you what you want to hear.

Evidence, not vibes.

Returns and risks come from NAV history. Peer comparisons and evidence behind the score are available to inspect.

Plain words, first.

Every metric starts with a simple explanation. The deeper definition is there when you want to understand more.

The core verdict is deterministic. A language model may help explain the result, but it does not decide Continue, Stop, or Shift.

Under the surface

Small specialists. One inspectable flow.

Built so the arithmetic stays reproducible and the optional language layer stays in its lane.

Coordinated work, not a black box.

FastAPI validates requests and hands fund tasks to a plan-driven LangGraph supervisor. Each specialist adds a known piece of the review; the supervisor itself uses no model to choose its next step.

  • Public data first. AMFI scheme identity and mfapi NAV history power the core.
  • Streaming progress. The browser sees review steps and each completed portfolio holding.
  • Optional services. Tavily and LLM access are isolated to research, market, insights, and narrative features.

The score isn't a secret

A verdict with its working attached.

Fund health is a weighted blend of peer standing, risk-adjusted returns, long-term returns, rolling consistency, and drawdown. Missing components are excluded and the remaining weights are renormalised.

Continue / Stop / Shift comes from documented rules.
Research claims display citations and source-reliability context.
Limits and educational-use caveats stay visible.
